Hi! I’m Alisya, a final-year MEng Mechanical Engineering with Nuclear Engineering student at Imperial College London.

I’m passionate about taking ideas from initial concepts through design and analysis to building and testing them in real life. Through the different projects I’ve taken on, I’ve modelled engineering systems across a range of disciplines, including thermal-hydraulics in Python, control systems in Simulink, engine fuel consumption in MATLAB, optical systems in Ansys OpticStudio, and structural analysis using Abaqus and SOLIDWORKS FEA.

I particularly enjoy engineering problems that expose me to different disciplines, whether that’s thermal-fluid and nuclear systems, dynamics and controls, or mechanical design. I’m always excited to apply what I’ve learned, explore unfamiliar areas and develop practical solutions to real-world engineering problems.
